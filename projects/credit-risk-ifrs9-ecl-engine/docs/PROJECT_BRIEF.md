# Project Brief

## Project Purpose

Create a GitHub-ready credit risk analytics project that demonstrates applied financial economics, Python analytics, machine learning, and IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss thinking.

## Target Roles

- Credit risk analyst
- Risk analytics analyst
- Financial data analyst
- Economic research analyst
- Fintech analytics analyst
- Banking technology analyst

## Target Companies

JPMorgan Chase, ING Hubs Philippines, Wells Fargo, GCash, Maya, BSP, P&G Philippines, MSCI, PwC, PIDS, AIA, UnionBank, BPI, KPMG, Manulife, BDO, and First Metro.

## MVP Scope

- Use a manually provided LendingClub-style loan dataset.
- Perform data understanding and cleaning.
- Build a simple probability of default workflow.
- Define transparent LGD and EAD assumptions.
- Calculate starter IFRS 9-style ECL outputs.
- Prepare a business memo, model card, and dashboard after outputs exist.

## Non-MVP Scope

- Production-grade model monitoring.
- Automated external data downloads.
- Real bank confidential data.
- Regulatory validation package.
- Advanced macroeconomic scenario modeling.

## Expected Outputs

- Cleaned analytical dataset.
- PD model artifacts.
- ECL results file.
- Business memo.
- Model card.
- Streamlit dashboard.
