# Resume Bullets

## General

- Built an end-to-end credit risk analytics portfolio project in Python, covering data understanding, baseline PD modeling, simplified IFRS 9-style ECL calculation, scenario analysis, and business-ready reporting.

## Banking Risk

- Developed a credit risk workflow using LendingClub-style loan data, logistic regression PD scores, simplified EAD/LGD assumptions, IFRS 9-style staging, and portfolio ECL summaries across 50,000 loans.

## Fintech

- Created a loan portfolio risk scoring and loss estimation prototype that converts borrower and loan attributes into PD scores, risk bands, stress scenarios, and dashboard-ready ECL summary tables.

## Risk Advisory

- Documented model assumptions, limitations, scenario impacts, and risk concentration findings in business memo and model card formats suitable for risk advisory and model governance discussions.

## Economic and Financial Research

- Analyzed credit portfolio loss sensitivity under base, mild stress, and severe stress assumptions, linking probability of default, loss given default, exposure, and portfolio concentration.

## Technical Analytics

- Implemented reusable Python modules and executable notebooks for data preparation, feature handling, scikit-learn logistic regression, ECL calculation, scenario analysis, and reporting outputs.
