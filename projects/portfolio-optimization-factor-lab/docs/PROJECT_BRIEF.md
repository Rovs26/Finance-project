# Project Brief

## Project Purpose

Create a GitHub-ready finance and investment analytics project that demonstrates market data workflows, return analysis, factor research, portfolio optimization, backtesting, and business interpretation.

## Target Roles

- Investment analyst
- Financial data analyst
- Market research analyst
- Corporate finance analyst
- Risk analytics analyst
- Quantitative research intern
- Fintech analytics analyst

## Target Companies

JPMorgan Chase, MSCI, Wells Fargo, P&G Philippines, BPI, First Metro, ING Hubs Philippines, PwC, and other finance or analytics employers.

## MVP Scope

- Use public market data first.
- Calculate asset and portfolio returns.
- Estimate CAPM and simple factor relationships.
- Build a basic portfolio optimization workflow.
- Backtest simple allocation strategies.
- Write a research memo and career-facing documentation.

## Non-MVP Scope

- Live trading.
- Automated production data pipelines.
- Portfolio dashboard.
- Advanced factor libraries.
- Transaction-cost-heavy institutional backtesting.
- Investment advice or recommendations.

## Expected Outputs

- Clean market return dataset.
- Factor exposure tables.
- Portfolio allocation outputs.
- Backtest results.
- Research memo.
- Career-facing report materials.
