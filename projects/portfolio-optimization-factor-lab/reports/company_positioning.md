# Company Positioning

## JPMorgan Chase

- Relevance: Connects market data, benchmark analysis, portfolio risk, and performance interpretation.
- Best angle: Finance analyst who can turn data into clear investment and risk insights.
- Tailored bullet: Built a Python portfolio analytics workflow comparing market sensitivity, optimized allocations, and fixed-weight strategy performance versus SPY.

## MSCI

- Relevance: Aligns with index analytics, factor exposure, benchmark-relative risk, and portfolio research.
- Best angle: Research analytics and factor interpretation.
- Tailored bullet: Estimated CAPM beta, benchmark correlation, rolling beta, and portfolio risk-return tradeoffs for a large-cap equity universe.

## Wells Fargo

- Relevance: Demonstrates financial analytics, risk measurement, and benchmark comparison.
- Best angle: Practical analytics project with transparent assumptions and controls.
- Tailored bullet: Created reusable Python notebooks and modules for return analytics, portfolio risk, and fixed-weight backtesting.

## P&G Philippines
