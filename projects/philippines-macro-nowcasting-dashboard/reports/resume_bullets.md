# Resume Bullets

Portfolio project: Philippines Macro Nowcasting and Policy Dashboard
Use the bullet most relevant to the target role. Tailor numbers and framing as needed.

---

## Macro Research Analyst (BSP, PIDS, research-focused roles)

Built an end-to-end Philippines inflation nowcasting workflow in Python — collected BSP and World Bank monthly macro data (1958-2026), engineered lag, rolling, and change features, compared three forecasting baselines on a chronological holdout, and interpreted results against the BSP 3.0% inflation target band, producing a reproducible research memo and scenario-based policy analysis.

---

## Banking Analytics Analyst (BPI, ING Hubs, JPMorgan-style)

Developed a Philippines macro nowcasting pipeline in Python covering 819 monthly inflation and USD/PHP observations; evaluated three baseline models on a chronological 80/20 split (best RMSE 0.4889pp); translated forecast outputs into bank NIM, fintech funding cost, and corporate refinancing scenario analysis relative to the BSP inflation target.

## BSP or PIDS Research Analyst Style

Constructed a reproducible Philippines macro research pipeline in Python using BSP historical inflation and exchange rate data (1958-2026); built three one-month-ahead inflation forecasting baselines; classified current inflation (4.1%, March 2026) and model forecast (5.02%, April 2026) relative to the BSP 2025-2028 target band; documented methodology, data sourcing, and limitations consistent with a policy research standard.

## JPMorgan or ING Macro Risk Analytics Style

Built a Philippines macroeconomic risk analytics workflow in Python — aggregated BSP and World Bank data, engineered time-series features (lags, rolling means, FX), benchmarked three inflation forecasting models (linear regression RMSE 0.4889pp vs naive RMSE 0.5002pp), and delivered a structured policy scenario analysis covering BSP hold/tighten, easing, and external shock scenarios for banking and corporate finance audiences.

---

## P&G or Corporate Finance Analytics Style

Developed a Philippines inflation monitoring and forecasting model in Python to support cost and working-capital planning scenarios; compared three baseline forecasting approaches on 66 years of monthly inflation data; translated model outputs into structured business implications for FX-exposed corporates under 4.5%-5.5% H1 2026 inflation scenarios; presented in an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

---

## Technical Python Analytics Style

Built a modular Python analytics pipeline (pandas, NumPy, Plotly, Streamlit) for Philippines macro nowcasting — covering data collection, feature engineering, time-series baseline forecasting, policy interpretation, and a five-page interactive Streamlit dashboard; all phases reproducible via Jupyter notebooks with documented outputs and limitations.
