# Project Brief

## Project Purpose

Create a GitHub-ready macroeconomics, finance, and policy analytics project focused on Philippines macro indicators, inflation monitoring, policy rate analysis, baseline nowcasting, and business interpretation.

## Target Roles

- Economic research analyst
- Macro research analyst
- Finance analytics analyst
- Banking analytics analyst
- Policy research analyst
- Business analytics analyst
- Fintech analytics analyst

## Target Companies

BSP, PIDS, JPMorgan Chase, ING Hubs Philippines, PwC Philippines, P&G Philippines, MSCI, Wells Fargo, BPI, and other finance or research employers.

## MVP Scope

- Collect or manually place public Philippines macro data.
- Focus first on inflation and policy rate analysis.
- Clean and standardize macro time series.
- Build simple features and baseline forecasts.
- Interpret results for policy, finance, and business audiences.
- Build dashboard only after clean outputs exist.

## Non-MVP Scope

- Production-grade official forecasting.
- High-frequency proprietary data feeds.
- Automated real-time policy monitoring.
- Advanced structural macroeconomic modeling.
- Investment, monetary policy, or business recommendations.

## Expected Outputs

- Clean macro indicator dataset.
- Feature table for nowcasting.
- Baseline forecast outputs.
- Scenario or policy interpretation tables.
- Streamlit dashboard after outputs exist.
- Research memo and career-facing documentation.
