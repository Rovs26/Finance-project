# Resume Bullets

## General Fixed Income Analytics

- Built a fixed income analytics project in Python that prices a synthetic bond portfolio, models coupon cash flows, calculates YTM, and summarizes portfolio market value.

## Treasury / Risk Analytics

- Implemented duration, convexity, DV01, and parallel interest-rate stress testing to estimate rate sensitivity and portfolio market value impact.

## Banking Balance Sheet Analytics

- Created an asset-side balance sheet sensitivity workflow that measures market value changes under `-100 bps` to `+200 bps` rate shock scenarios.

## Insurance / AIA / Manulife

- Modeled fixed income cash flows and interest-rate sensitivity for a synthetic bond book, with outputs relevant to insurer asset portfolios and ALM discussion.

## MSCI / Research Analytics

- Developed transparent fixed income research outputs including bond-level price, yield, duration, convexity, DV01, sector concentration, and scenario loss summaries.

## Technical Python Analytics

- Built reusable Python modules for bond cash flows, pricing, risk metrics, stress scenarios, and visualization, with executed Jupyter notebooks and reproducible CSV outputs.
