# Fixed Income and Balance Sheet Analytics Platform

Fixed income portfolio project for pricing synthetic bonds, modeling coupon cash flows, and building a foundation for treasury and balance sheet risk analytics.

**Disclaimer:** The bond book in this project is synthetic and used only for portfolio learning. It is not real bank, insurer, or client data.

## Business Problem

Banks, insurers, and treasury teams need tools to understand bond cash flows, pricing, yield-to-maturity, and portfolio market value. Before stress testing or balance sheet risk analytics, the project needs a clear pricing engine and auditable bond-level outputs.

## Limitations

- Synthetic bond book only.
- Simple Actual/365 day-count assumption.
- Flat bond-level market yield; no real yield curve yet.
- No accrued interest convention or settlement calendar.
- Parallel rate shocks only.
- No credit spread, liquidity, optionality, or liability-side modeling yet.
- No dashboard yet.
