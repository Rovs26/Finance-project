# Project Brief

## Purpose

Build a fixed income analytics portfolio project that demonstrates bond cash flow modeling, bond pricing, yield-to-maturity calculation, and the foundation for treasury or balance sheet risk analytics.

## Target Roles

- Fixed income analyst
- Treasury analyst
- Risk analytics analyst
- Balance sheet analytics analyst
- Investment analytics analyst
- Financial data analyst

## Target Companies

JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, ING Hubs Philippines, AIA Philippines, BSP, MSCI, BPI, First Metro, Manulife, and PwC Philippines.

## MVP Scope

- Synthetic bond book
- Coupon cash flow generation
- Bond pricing by discounted cash flow
- Yield-to-maturity calculation
- Portfolio market value summary
- Starter figures and documentation

## Non-MVP Scope

- Real market yield curve construction
- Duration and convexity
- Stress testing
- IFRS or ALM accounting treatment
- Dashboard
- Production treasury system

## Expected Outputs

- Synthetic bond book CSV
- Bond cash flow table
- Bond pricing results
- Portfolio pricing summary
- Figures for pricing by rating, sector value, cash flows, and price-yield relationship
