# Project Brief

## Purpose

Turn an existing financial news sentiment and market connectedness prototype into a clean, auditable, GitHub-ready research project.

## Target Roles

- Market research analyst
- Finance analytics analyst
- Risk analytics analyst
- Investment data analyst
- Fintech analytics analyst
- Data analyst

## Target Companies

JPMorgan Chase, MSCI, ING Hubs Philippines, Wells Fargo, First Metro, BPI, PwC Philippines, KPMG Philippines, fintech analytics teams, and market research teams.

## MVP Scope

- Preserve expected prototype script structure.
- Audit available raw files.
- Inspect schema, date coverage, and company coverage.
- Prepare reusable modules for ingestion, sentiment audit, market audit, merging, and connectedness input inspection.
- Execute a notebook-first project audit.

## Non-MVP Scope

- Heavy scraping
- Paid APIs
- Trading recommendations
- Backtested trading performance claims
- Final connectedness modeling
- Dashboard

## Expected Outputs

- File inventory
- Legacy script inventory
- Schema summary
- Coverage summary
- Company coverage summary
- Audit figures where data supports them
