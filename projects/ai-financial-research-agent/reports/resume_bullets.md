# Resume Bullets

- Built a local AI financial research prototype in Python that ingests sample finance documents, chunks text, retrieves evidence with TF-IDF, and creates cited memo outputs without paid APIs.
- Developed a fintech risk intelligence workflow that flags credit, liquidity, adoption, operational, market, inflation, rate, and profitability risks from retrieved evidence.
- Created a banking research automation pipeline with document-level metadata, chunk-level citations, evidence tables, and grounding checks for traceable analyst review.
- Designed a risk advisory-style review process that maps research questions to cited evidence and summarizes risk themes for consulting or audit analytics use cases.
- Built an MSCI-style research analytics prototype that links market, portfolio, macro, and profitability themes to traceable source snippets.
- Implemented Python NLP utilities for ingestion, chunking, TF-IDF retrieval, citation mapping, rule-based risk flags, evaluation outputs, and visualization.
